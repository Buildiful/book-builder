Analytics Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Buildiful® offers free tools that work whether or not you allow optional analytics.

Optional analytics

With your permission, we load Google Analytics 4 to understand which tools and pages are visited. Google Analytics may use cookies and process page visits, approximate location, and browser and device information. We configure our analytics tag not to use Google signals or advertising personalization.

We do not intentionally send your manuscripts, designs, generated file contents, or other creations to Google Analytics. Our tag records page paths without URL search parameters or fragments.

Your choice

Declining optional analytics prevents our Google Analytics script from loading. We save your choice and its timestamp in your browser's local storage so you do not need to answer again on tools served from the same origin. You can reopen the choice through the “Analytics settings” control in a participating tool. Clearing browser storage may reset it.

Changing your choice to decline stops new collection on subsequent page loads. Data already recorded before consent was withdrawn is not automatically removed from Google Analytics.

More information and contact

See Google's Privacy Policy for details on Google's processing.

For privacy questions or data-related requests, please use the contact options on Buildiful's official website.

This notice describes optional analytics only; other services used by a tool may have separate data practices.
